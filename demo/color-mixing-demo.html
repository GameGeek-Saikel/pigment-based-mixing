The model

A two-stage formula: a K–M/Duncan mixing core plus a repair layer. The core applies a broadband 3×3 matrix, analytic K/S defaults, κ concentration mixing, mutual absorption and a rational R∞; the repair layer adds a soft role assignment λ, a dark-plus-light gate g, a per-channel power-law target and a seventeen-feature harmonic correction. No trigonometric function, no lookup table.

The comparisons

RGB interpolation is additive light mixing, so complementary colors go gray. Display-value K–M treats channel values as reflectances, so dark colors washed with white lose absorption. Spectral truth comes from 29 synthetic pigment spectra (two-constant K–M; band structure from pigment chemistry, numbers calibrated to the public BabelColor ColorChecker spectra) through Duncan superposition and D65 integration, after a deterministic unmix of the RGB input into four pigment concentrations. Mixbox 2.0 mixes linearly in a seven-dimensional latent space trained on measured pigment spectra and is the reference here.

Reading it

ΔE00 below 1 is imperceptible, 1–2 visible on close inspection, 2–4 clearly visible, above 4 substantial. Our model measures 2.26 mean / 7.3 max on dark + white, and 3.29 / 2.67 / 2.78 on the three main suites. Full statistics and the held-out split are on the evaluation page and in the paper.
